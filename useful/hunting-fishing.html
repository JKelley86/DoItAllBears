What Super Sports covers
Individual Super Sports: angling, small game, trout/salmon validation, pheasant validation, state waterfowl validation, and one deer license choice. HIP and the Federal Duck Stamp remain separate when applicable.

Combination Super Sports: married-couple angling and two trout validations, with small game, state waterfowl, pheasant and one deer license choice for the primary purchaser only. HIP and Federal Duck Stamp remain separate when applicable.

Duck Season
Dates: Sept 27 – Oct 5 / Oct 11 - Nov 30
Requirements: State Waterfowl Stamp, Federal Duck Stamp, Small Game License, HIP Certification
Notes: Steel shot only. Can only have 3 shells at one time. Must have a plug.

Dates: Sept 27 – Oct 5 / Oct 11 - Dec 31

Pheasant Season
Dates: Sept 27 – Oct 5 / Oct 11 - Dec 31
Requirements: Pheasant Stamp, Small Game License
Notes: Steel shot only. Must wear one blaze orange article of clothing above the waist.
Shooting Hours: Half hour before sunrise to sunset.
